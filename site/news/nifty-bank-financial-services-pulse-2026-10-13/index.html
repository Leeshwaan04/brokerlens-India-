The financial services cluster on the National Stock Exchange accounts for the largest weighting in the benchmark Nifty 50. In recent sessions, institutional flows have reflected a focus on net interest margins (NIMs) and credit expansion across private and public sector lenders.

Structural Drivers for Indian Banking

  • Credit Expansion: Systemic bank credit growth continues at healthy double-digit rates, supported by retail personal loans, auto finance, and corporate working capital.
  • Asset Quality: Gross Non-Performing Assets (GNPA) across commercial banks remain at multi-year lows, supported by stringent SARFAESI and IBC resolution frameworks.
  • Deposit Competition: Banks continue to offer competitive term deposit rates to match ongoing retail credit appetite.
