Grace Cancer Foundation - ZCZP IPO Live Status: Subscribed 0.02x, Price Band Rs.10

Researched by BrokerLens Financial Research Desk
Fact-Checked & Verified against primary NSE, BSE and SEBI disclosures

The initial public offering of Grace Cancer Foundation - ZCZP (GCFZCZP) is open for public bidding on Indian exchanges. As per latest NSE disclosures, the issue has received bids for 0.02x of the total shares offered.

UPI Mandate Cutoff: 5:00 PM

How to Apply Online via UPI

  1. Log into your registered Demat account on your broker's trading platform (Zerodha Kite, Groww, 5paisa, Angel One).
  2. Navigate to the IPO tab and locate Grace Cancer Foundation - ZCZP.
  3. Enter your desired lot size and bid price (selecting the cut-off price is standard practice for retail investors).
  4. Enter your UPI ID (Google Pay, PhonePe, BHIM, or bank UPI handle) and submit the application.
  5. Approve the UPI mandate block request on your banking app before 5:00 PM on 09 October 2026.

Full IPO Filing: View issue structure, registrar information, and issue dates on the Grace Cancer Foundation - ZCZP IPO Detailed Page or track all upcoming issues on the IPO Tracker.
