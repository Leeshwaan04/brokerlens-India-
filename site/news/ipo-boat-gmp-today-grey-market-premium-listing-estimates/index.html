Kostak Rate and Subject to Sauda

The Kostak rate for Imagine Marketing Limited (boAt) IPO is quoted at Rs 350 per retail application. Kostak represents the fixed profit an applicant locks in by trading their IPO application rights before allotment. Investors should note that trading in the unofficial grey market is subject to counterparty risk as transactions are not settled or guaranteed by SEBI, NSE, or BSE.
