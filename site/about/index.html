About BrokerLens

BrokerLens is an independent financial data and research infrastructure platform dedicated to Indian retail investors. We analyze stock brokers, listed equities, mutual fund schemes, ETFs, and public issues (IPOs) using verified empirical data derived directly from regulatory filings and exchange infrastructure feeds.

Our Mission & Purpose

In an era dominated by promotional finfluencer endorsements, sponsored marketing awards, and hidden transaction costs, BrokerLens was built to establish a transparent, mathematically rigorous benchmark for the Indian capital markets. We believe retail investors deserve clear, objective facts before committing their hard-earned capital.

What We Track

  • SEBI-Registered Intermediaries: Comprehensive directory of over 1,600 recognized stock brokers, depository participants (CDSL and NSDL), merchant bankers, and portfolio managers.
  • Equities & Market Microstructure: Real-time daily prices, candlestick charts, and corporate actions across active NSE and BSE listed stocks and benchmark indices.
  • Mutual Funds & ETFs: Performance analytics, portfolio expense ratios, and historical NAV trends across AMFI-registered mutual fund schemes and index ETFs.
  • Initial Public Offerings (IPOs): Audited Red Herring Prospectus (RHP) balance sheet disclosures, retail lot sizing limits, live bidding demand, and verified post-listing gains.
  • True Cost Calculators: Mathematical trade cost engines auditing brokerage, STT, exchange turnover charges, SEBI turnover fees, stamp duty, and GST down to the exact paisa.

Editorial & Research Team

Our research is produced and curated by the BrokerLens Financial Research Desk, composed of financial software engineers, quantitative analysts, and regulatory compliance reviewers. Our team specializes in Indian market microstructure, clearing corporation settlement rules, and SEBI ICDR/LODR compliance.

We do not accept subjective opinions or promotional marketing copy. Every calculation is automated via audited Python algorithms, and every published report is checked against official circulars before release.

Verification Architecture & Methodology

Every figure published across BrokerLens traces to an immutable, named primary source: NSE, BSE, SEBI, AMFI, RBI, or the Ministry of Corporate Affairs. Our data ingestion pipelines run automated sanity sweeps, verifying active client counts, exchange complaints ratios, and margin rules.

Review the mathematical mechanics in our Methodology Page and inspect the full cadence and status of every feed on our Primary Sources Directory.

What BrokerLens Deliberately Does Not Do

BrokerLens is an informational and research publisher, not a SEBI-registered Investment Adviser (IA) or Research Analyst (RA). Nothing on this platform constitutes investment advice, financial planning, portfolio management, or a buy/sell stock recommendation.

We do not operate a proprietary trading desk, sell advisory tip packages, manage client funds, or publish speculative grey market premiums (GMP). Certain pages contain clearly designated affiliate links to leading SEBI-registered brokers. These partnerships help finance our infrastructure but never influence our ratings, comparison tables, or objective reviews.
