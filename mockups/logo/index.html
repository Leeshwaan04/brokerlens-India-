01

Lens on the line

Recommended

A single lens ring holds a rising trend line in focus: bold and green inside the glass, thin and faint where it leaves it. The metaphor is the product itself: the market is noisy, and the part BrokerLens examines becomes legible. At 26px it reduces to exactly two shapes, a ring and a rising line, so nothing is lost in the nav. There is deliberately no handle: this is an analyst's lens, not a search icon.

02

Aperture monogram

A geometric B assembled from optics: two tangent lens elements sharing one axis on a stem, with a bent green uptick sitting inside the larger glass. It keeps a monogram's instant brand recall while replacing typography with construction, so the mark stays text-free. Compact and confident, though at favicon size the two bowls begin to read as a figure 8.

03

Focus bracket

Camera focus brackets frame a compact rising chart: the market, held in focus. It is the most literal measurement image of the three, feels native next to data tables, and survives one-color reduction perfectly. Its weakness is density: below 20px the gaps between the bars start to close and the mark loses its rhythm.

Recommendation: ship Concept 1

Lens on the line is the only concept where the name, the product, and the mark say the same thing in half a second: the market is blurry until BrokerLens looks at it. It wins the 26px test with just two shapes, holds a 16px tab, and its one-color version keeps the focus story intact. Concept 3 is a worthy secondary device for social cards and report covers, where its density has room. Concept 2 can be retired without loss.

On the gradient: the placeholder's 140deg indigo to green blend averages into a muddy teal below 32px and cannot survive one-color reduction, so the new marks use flat inks: indigo structure with a single green accent. Two colors, zero gradients, one asset from favicon to hero.

Implementation: drop the SVG into .brand-mark in site/index.html at 26px and remove the gradient tile background (the mark sits directly on the header, no container). For the favicon, serialize the same symbol with the light-theme fallback inks into the existing data URI, and keep the pullback in the line pronounced: a lens with a plain diagonal can scan as a cancel glyph at 16px, the deep kink is what prevents that. Colors are wired to --lg-a and --lg-b with hex fallbacks, so the markup works verbatim inside the production CSP with no external assets.
